AI Visibility Audit

An AI Visibility Audit answers one question: when your customers ask an AI engine for the best business in your category, does your name come up? We test it directly, benchmark you against named competitors, scan the technical signals engines rely on, and hand you a scored report with a prioritised fix list. It's free, and the report is yours whoever you hire.

Get a free AI Visibility Audit Talk to Farhan

What the audit uncovers

  • Whether four major AI engines mention you at all for the questions your buyers actually ask — and which competitor they name instead.
  • The technical reasons: missing structured data, broken crawl files, thin content, no llms.txt, headings with no query intent.
  • The gap between your brand queries (which usually work) and your category queries (which usually don't) — the second is where buying decisions happen.

What you receive

01

Four engines tested live

ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Claude asked your real customer questions, with screenshots of what each returned.

02

Named competitor benchmark

The same questions run for two to three competitors so you can see exactly who is being recommended in your place, and why.

03

Technical extractability scan

Schema, crawlability, answer-readiness, sitemap, robots and llms.txt — the signals AI engines need before they will trust a source.

04

Scored report and fix list

A visibility score out of 100 across four categories, and a prioritised action plan ordered by impact rather than effort.

How to read the score

Below 40 means effectively invisible — engines have almost nothing to extract or cite. Between 40 and 60 means visibility at risk: readable by humans, weak for machines. Between 60 and 80 means half-visible, with foundations in place but a thin extraction layer. Above 80 means AI-ready. Our published sample report scored a real site at 38 out of 100, with every finding evidenced.

Frequently asked questions

How much does the AI Visibility Audit cost?

It is free with no obligation. The report is yours to keep and act on, whether or not you work with us.

How long does it take?

Three business days from the time you send your website and the category you want to be found for.

What do you need from me?

Your website URL, and the question a customer would type to find a business like yours — for example "best dental clinic in Auckland". That's it.

Is this just an automated scan?

No. The technical checks are systematic, but the engine testing is run manually with your real customer questions and the competitor benchmark is hand-selected. You can see the depth in our published sample report.

What happens after the audit?

Nothing automatic. You get the report and the fix list. If you want us to implement it we'll quote for that separately, and if you'd rather hand it to your own developer, that works too.
