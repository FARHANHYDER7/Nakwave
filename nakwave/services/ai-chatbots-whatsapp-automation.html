Frequently asked questions

Will the chatbot make things up?

Not if it's built correctly. We ground it in your real data with explicit guardrails, so when it doesn't know something it hands over to a human rather than inventing an answer. That constraint is the difference between an asset and a liability.

Is this the official WhatsApp Business API?

Yes. We deploy on the official API with proper template approval. Unofficial workarounds risk your number being banned, which is an expensive way to save a little setup time.

Can it hand over to a human?

Always. You define the escalation rules — value thresholds, sensitive topics, explicit customer request — and the human receives the full transcript so the customer never repeats themselves.

What can it actually do beyond answering?

Check stock, qualify budget, value a trade-in, calculate payments, offer real appointment slots, take the booking and score the lead for your morning follow-up list.
