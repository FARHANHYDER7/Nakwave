Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) Services

Answer Engine Optimization is the work of structuring your website so AI engines extract, trust and cite it when someone asks a question. Unlike SEO, which competes for a position in a list of ten links, AEO competes to be the single answer. Why ranking is no longer enough

  • Searches that trigger an AI Overview end without a click about 83% of the time. The answer is the destination — the click never happens.
  • Roughly 5 in 6 AI-Overview citations come from pages outside the classic top 10. Your Google position does not decide whether an AI quotes you.
  • That cuts both ways: if you've been stuck on page two for years, AEO is the first channel where you can leapfrog bigger competitors without out-spending them on backlinks.

What an AEO engagement includes

01

Structured data build

Organization, LocalBusiness, Service, Product, FAQPage and Article JSON-LD deployed sitewide. This is how engines confirm who you are and what you sell — most sites we audit have none of it.

02

Answer-first content rewrites

Every key page rewritten to answer its core question in the first two sentences, in self-contained passages an engine can lift cleanly without the surrounding context.

03

Entity & E-E-A-T signals

Consistent naming, author bylines with real credentials, Person schema and sameAs links so engines resolve you as a specific, real entity rather than an unnamed website.

04

Crawl layer for AI bots

robots.txt that explicitly welcomes GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot and Google-Extended, a valid sitemap, and an llms.txt manifest summarising your business for AI crawlers.

05

Citation tracking

Monthly testing across ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Claude with your real customer questions, benchmarked against named competitors, so you can see movement rather than assume it.

Frequently asked questions

What is Answer Engine Optimization?

Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) is the practice of structuring a website so AI engines — ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude and Google's AI Overviews — can extract, trust and cite its content when answering user questions. Where SEO competes for a ranking position, AEO competes to be the answer itself.

How is AEO different from SEO?

They share a foundation — crawlability, quality content, trust signals — but differ at the finish line. SEO optimises for ranking in a list of results. AEO optimises for citation: being the passage an AI engine extracts and repeats. A page can rank poorly and still be cited, or rank well and never be quoted.

How long does AEO take to show results?

Structured data and crawl-file changes are read within days to weeks of deployment. Content and entity signals compound over one to three months. We report from your own Search Console and monthly AI-engine testing rather than estimating.

Does AEO work for a local business?

Yes, and often faster than for national brands. Local queries are among the most zero-click-resilient — people still act on them. LocalBusiness schema with hours and reviews, consistent name/address/phone, and answer-first local pages are what decide which business an AI names.

Can you prove the work?

Every engagement ships with a before and after visibility score, the specific fixes applied, and screenshots of AI-engine responses for your money questions. See our measured rebuild case study for the full method.
