What usually holds a site back

  • No structured data at all, which leaves engines guessing what the business is and what it sells.
  • Thin pages of a few hundred words competing against comprehensive, well-linked competitor pages.
  • Broken or missing crawl files — a robots.txt returning an error page, no sitemap, deep pages nothing links to.
  • Decorative headings with no query intent, so the most valuable extraction real estate on the page says nothing a buyer would search.

Frequently asked questions

How long does SEO take to work?

For a new domain, meaningful organic traffic typically takes three to six months, because search engines apply a trust and tenure factor no technique bypasses. Technical fixes register within days; content and authority compound over months. Anyone promising page one in week one is selling something.

Do you do SEO and AEO separately?

No, and separating them wastes money. The structured data, crawl hygiene and answer-first content that win AI citations are the same foundations that lift traditional rankings. We build both in one pass.

Will you rebuild my site or fix the existing one?

Whichever is cheaper for the outcome. If the foundations are sound we fix in place. If the platform fights every improvement, a static rebuild is usually faster and permanently cheaper to run.

Do you guarantee rankings?

No, and treat any agency that does with suspicion — nobody controls Google's index. What we guarantee is the work: the specific fixes, deployed and verified, with before-and-after measurement from your own Search Console.
