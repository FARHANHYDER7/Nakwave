Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 July 2026

Nakwave ("we", "us") is an SEO and AI-automation studio run by Farhan Hyder. This policy explains what information we collect when you use this website, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We keep it deliberately short and plain.

What we collect

  • Information you give us through forms. When you request an audit or contact us, we collect the details you enter — typically your name, email address, website URL and any message or context you provide.
  • Basic usage data. Like most websites, we may use privacy-respecting analytics to understand how many people visit and which pages they view. This is aggregated and is not used to identify you personally.
  • We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal data, payment card details, or information from children.

How we use it

  • To respond to your audit or contact request and deliver what you asked for.
  • To follow up about relevant services, only when you have reached out to us.
  • To improve the website and understand what content is useful.

How forms are processed

Our forms are handled by FormSubmit, a third-party form-delivery service that forwards submissions to our email inbox. Your submission passes through FormSubmit's systems for that purpose; please see their own privacy terms for details. We receive and store the message in our email.

Analytics & cookies

If analytics are enabled, they may set cookies or similar identifiers to measure aggregate traffic. We do not use advertising trackers or sell data to advertisers. You can block cookies in your browser settings without losing access to the site's content.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent your information. We only share it with the service providers needed to run the site and respond to you (for example, our form and email providers), and where required by law.

Retention

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to respond and maintain our records of the conversation, and no longer than necessary. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

Your choices

  • Ask what information we hold about you, or request that we correct or delete it.
  • Unsubscribe from any follow-up at any time.
  • Decline cookies via your browser.

Contact
