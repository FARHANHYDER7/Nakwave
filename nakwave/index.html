Both, with equal weight — they're the studio's two crafts. The search half is classic expertise: keyword research, technical SEO, on-page optimization, plus AEO that gets you cited by AI engines. The automation half is engineering: AI chatbots, WhatsApp bots, multi-agent workflows and dashboards, built in-house. Hire either one — most clients end up using both, because visibility without automation leaks leads, and automation without visibility has nothing to work with.

AI visibility is whether AI engines like ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Claude mention, cite or recommend your brand when customers ask them questions. Nearly a third of consumers now use AI search instead of Google. If AI doesn't know your business exists, those customers go to whichever competitor it recommends instead.

AEO is the practice of structuring your website content so AI engines can extract, cite and recommend it. It includes answer-first page formatting, FAQ and JSON-LD schema markup, llms.txt files, and self-contained passages that fully answer a customer question — making your pages the source AI pulls answers from.

SEO optimizes for ranking positions in a list of blue links. AEO optimizes for being the single answer an AI engine gives. They share foundations — clean technical structure, quality content, trust signals — but AEO adds extractable answer formatting, structured data depth and citation tracking across AI platforms. You need both: we deliver them together.
