What the audit caught, and what we did.

Twenty real issues, each paired with the exact change in the rebuild. This is the kind of list we hand you for your own site.

No JSON-LD anywhere — engines had nothing to confirm who or what this is.		✓Added JSON-LD across the page — the single biggest fix.

No viewport tag — a hard demotion in every modern index.		✓Added the viewport meta and rebuilt the layout mobile-first.
