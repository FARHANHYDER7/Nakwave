01 — Why it exists

The lead goes cold before anyone wakes up.

A luxury car buyer fills in an enquiry at 11pm. At most dealerships, nothing happens until a salesperson logs in the next morning — and by then the buyer has messaged two other showrooms. The gap between "I'm interested" and "someone replied" is where deals quietly die.

The industry data is blunt about it. Mystery-shop studies put dealer response rates in a harsh light — and the reward for closing that gap is well documented.

  • Only 13.2% of dealerships reply to an internet lead within 5 minutes; most take an hour or more
  • Roughly 1 in 3 dealer leads never receive any reply at all
  • 391% higher close rate when the first contact happens inside 60 seconds
  • After-hours enquiries — evenings and weekends — are where the most leads leak

Figures from published automotive mystery-shop and lead-response studies (Velocify, 2026 industry reports). Shown here as industry context for the concept — not results from a named client.

02 — What the AI employee does

It works the floor while the floor is closed.

01

Reads the enquiry, not a keyword

Ask for "a white electric SUV under 80k" and it searches a real 18-car luxury inventory — Porsche, Mercedes, BMW, Audi, Lexus, Range Rover and more — matching body, fuel, colour and budget, then talks back about what it found.

02

Values the trade-in on the spot

Give it a make, year, mileage and condition and it returns a real valuation range — a depreciation model, not a lookup: brand tier, age decay and a mileage penalty all factor in, with an honest low–high band instead of a single fake number.

03

Runs the finance math backwards

Buyers think in monthly payments, not sticker prices. Tell it a monthly budget and a deposit and it solves for the car you can actually afford over a 60-month term — the same payment math a finance manager would run.

04

Books the test drive & scores the lead

It offers real open slots across the next few days, takes the booking, and scores the lead by intent, budget, trade and finance readiness — so the morning briefing to Sofia, the human closer, is ranked hottest-first. The night's work is done before the showroom lights come on.

03 — The engine inside

Every number is computed, not scripted.

Inventory NLU

Parses free-text enquiries into body, fuel, colour and budget filters, then queries a live 18-unit luxury inventory for genuine matches.

Trade-in valuation

A depreciation model — brand tier, age decay and mileage penalty — returns a defensible low–high range, not a made-up figure.

Finance solver

Payment-to-budget math over a 60-month term: give it a monthly figure and deposit, it solves for the reachable car price.

Live test-drive booking

Generates real open slots across the coming days and confirms the appointment inside the conversation.

Lead scoring

Scores each lead on intent, budget, trade and finance signals so the human team gets a ranked, hottest-first queue.

Morning briefing

Compiles the night's conversations into a ranked handover for Sofia — who talked, what they want, what to do next.

Provably consistent math

The scroll-story and the live chat call the exact same valuation and finance functions — the numbers can't disagree.

Cinematic scroll-story

A pinned narrative of one night, 23:47 to 08:56, with the trade counter computed by the same engine driving the chat.

04 — What's real and what's a concept

No theatre, no fake client.

Meridian the dealership is invented — a stage to show the work on. We don't present it as a paying client, and there are no real customer names or numbers anywhere in it. That line matters to us.

The engine is real, though. The inventory search, the depreciation valuation, the finance solver, the slot booking and the lead scoring are working code you can poke at until it breaks. When we deploy this for an actual dealership, only the inventory, branding and CRM wiring change — the intelligence is already built.
