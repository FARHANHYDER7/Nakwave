01 — What was needed

The owner's Monday morning problem.

The brief came from a familiar place: a well-run boutique hotel with healthy occupancy — and no single view of the business. Reservations lived in the channel manager, revenue in accounting exports, OTA commissions in a spreadsheet nobody trusted, and the weekly "how are we doing?" answer took a manager half a day to assemble every Monday.

What they asked for, in their words: "One screen that tells us how the hotel is actually doing — without anyone building reports. And when something looks off, we want to know before the end of the month, not after."

  • One live view of revenue, bookings, guests and booking value
  • Channel mix visibility — how much of the business OTAs really own
  • Warnings when a week behaves abnormally, without anyone checking
  • A forecast the owner can plan staffing and purchasing against
  • The Monday report — written automatically
02 — What we delivered

An analyst that never sleeps.

01

Unified data pipeline

Booking exports drop in as CSV — the cleaning agent maps the columns, normalises dates, strips duplicates and logs every fix it made. Messy channel-manager exports become one trusted dataset, no manual prep.

02

Live KPI dashboard

Revenue, bookings, guests and average booking value with period-on-period deltas, weekly revenue analytics, channel mix, and top revenue sources across rooms and services — deluxe kings to breakfast add-ons.

03

The AI layer

This is where the analyst lives: statistical anomaly alerts on every week of trading, a 12-week trend×seasonality forecast with honest confidence bands, guest RFM segmentation with suggested retention moves, and an insight feed written by the engine from the numbers themselves.

04

Automation that answers

A copilot that computes answers from live data — "why did revenue spike?", "best room type?" — a what-if simulator for rate and marketing decisions, and the Monday owner report generated in one click, print-ready.

03 — The AI & automation inside

Every tool in the box, and what it does.

Anomaly detection

Z-score scan (σ ≥ 2.1) across every trading week — flags spikes and dips with severity before a human would notice.

Revenue forecasting

Trend × seasonality model fitted to 26 weeks of history, projecting 12 weeks ahead with an 80% confidence band.

NLG insight engine

Turns computations into written findings — strongest day, channel concentration risk, fastest-rising room type.

Computed-answer copilot

Ask in plain language; it aggregates the live dataset and answers with numbers, not canned text.

Guest segmentation

RFM scoring sorts guests into six segments — champions to at-risk — each with a suggested retention play.

CSV cleaning agent

Column mapping, date normalisation, dedupe and type coercion — with a transparent log of every fix.

What-if simulation

Rate elasticity and diminishing-return ad models let the owner test decisions before spending a dirham.

Report generation

The Monday report, written by the engine: KPIs, channels, anomalies, outlook and recommended actions.
