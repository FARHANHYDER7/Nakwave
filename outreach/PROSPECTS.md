## HOW TO SEND (read this before you send anything)

**Volume and pace.** Send **10–12 a day, spread out**, never a bulk blast. Gmail will land you in spam if you fire 44 at once from a personal account. At 12/day you're through the whole list in four days.

**Format.** Plain text. No images, no logo, no HTML signature, no tracking pixel. It should look like a person typed it, because a person did.

**Links.** One link maximum in the first email — your site in the signature. More than that trips spam filters on a cold send.

**Send time.** 10–11am or 3–4pm IST, Tuesday to Thursday. Avoid Monday morning and anything after 6pm.

**Subject lines.** Lowercase, specific, no marketing words. "Free audit" and "Grow your business" go straight to spam. The subjects below are written that way on purpose — don't dress them up.

**Reply-to.** Send from `hyderfarhan96@gmail.com` and reply from the same. Don't use a no-reply or an alias.

## THE 10-MINUTE PRE-SEND RITUAL (this is what makes it land)

Before each email, do this. It takes two minutes per prospect and it is the difference between a template and a letter.

1. **Open their site.** Confirm the thing I found is still true. Sites change; my audit is a snapshot from 31 July 2026.
2. **Open ChatGPT and paste this**, swapping in their category and area:
   > *"I'm looking for the best [cosmetic dental clinic] in [Domlur, Bangalore]. Give me your top 5 with a sentence on each."*
3. **Screenshot the answer.** If they're not in it — and most won't be — that screenshot is the single most persuasive thing you own. **Do not describe what ChatGPT said unless you have actually run it.** If you claim it and they check and you're wrong, you're finished with that prospect.
4. **Attach nothing.** Mention the screenshot, offer to send it. Attachments on a cold email hurt deliverability.
5. **Log it in `tracker.csv`** — date sent, follow-up due (+4 days). If it's not in the tracker it didn't happen. This is what went wrong last time.
